Walltaker Favorites Widget

A widget that lets you display a random selection of posts from your e621 favorites on your Walltaker profile. The posts are randomized everytime the widget is loaded.

⚠ WTFW is still in development. Widgets generated right now may stop working in the future.

Autofill from widget code If you'd like to change your widget's settings through WTFW's generator, you can paste in your current widget (iframe only !) here, click on 'Autofill', change what you want, and Generate the widget again.
