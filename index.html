Walltaker Favorites Widget

A widget that lets you display a random selection of posts from your e621 favorites. The posts are randomized everytime the element is loaded.

Features
  • You can apply custom width and height to the widget via CSS, and everything should still display correctly. The preview box here is resizable (there should be a small handle on the bottom right corner), give it a shot !
  • You can click on the images to copy the e621 link to your clipboard
